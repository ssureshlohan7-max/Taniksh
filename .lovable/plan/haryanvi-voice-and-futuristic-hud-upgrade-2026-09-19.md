# Haryanvi voice and futuristic HUD upgrade

## What will change
- Make speech recognition listen in Indian Hindi so Haryanvi commands are understood more reliably.
- Make spoken replies prefer an available Hindi voice, with slower, more natural delivery.
- Guide JARVIS to answer in simple, respectful local Haryanvi when the user speaks that way.
- Add a compact language control for Haryanvi/Hindi and English.
- Enhance the existing yellow-and-black Iron Man HUD with sharper frames, layered targeting rings, telemetry strips, glow depth, and clearer active states.

## Technical details
- Keep browser speech and the existing Gemini connection; no extra paid voice provider is added.
- Preserve website launching, wake word, photo editing, and all current controls.
- Verify the refreshed screen on desktop and mobile and check the live build output.

## Limitation
- The exact accent depends on Hindi voices installed by the phone or browser. The app will automatically choose the best available Hindi voice.
